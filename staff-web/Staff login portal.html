SwiftQ · Staff web app

Kgomotso Seanego

Builds the Staff web app for SwiftQ, the digital queue system for public clinics. Receptionists, nurses and managers use it to run the live queue: call the next patient, mark no-shows, add walk-ins and keep the day's queue in order.

What Kgomotso owns

The screen staff keep open all day

The Staff web app is the web counterpart of the clinic floor: one dashboard for the live queue, with the controls staff need to move patients through it.

W2 · W9

Live queue dashboard

Queue list with neutral priority indicators, plus patient search on the dashboard.

W3

Call next and no-show

Call the next patient, mark a no-show, or dismiss a ticket.

W4

Manual walk-in add

Add a patient who arrives without having joined from the phone app.

W5

Priority and queue control

Confirm priority, pause or resume the queue, and transfer a ticket.

W8

Queue CRUD module

Open the day's queue, edit it, and close or delete it at end of day, with validation and toasts.

W1 · W6 · W7

Access, sync and accessibility

Firebase login with receptionist, nurse and manager role guards; a sync health indicator; keyboard, contrast and focus-order pass.
